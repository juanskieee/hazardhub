About Us

Hazard Hub is a comprehensive workplace safety management platform designed to help organizations identify, track, and resolve hazards and safety concerns efficiently.

Our system enables employees and administrators to report incidents, monitor fire protection equipment, and manage safety documentation — all in one centralized hub.

Built with a commitment to safety excellence, Hazard Hub streamlines the entire safety management workflow from hazard identification to resolution.
